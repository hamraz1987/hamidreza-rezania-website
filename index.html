Bio

Hamidreza Rezania is a polymer and membrane technology leader based in Calgary, Alberta, with more than 10 years of experience across industrial R&D, applied academia, and commercialization.

He is PMP certified and specializes in translating advanced polymer science into scalable membrane systems for solvent separations, carbon capture, water treatment, desalination, and emerging industrial separation applications.

Research Summary

Hamidreza's work focuses on polymeric membrane platforms and structure-property-performance relationships for industrial separations. His experience spans membrane fabrication, modification, module concepts, cross-flow filtration systems, pilot-scale validation, IP development, and commercialization assessment.

His recent industrial R&D has included solvent-resistant membrane platforms, organic solvent nanofiltration, PFAS removal, beer dealcoholization and diafiltration, wastewater treatment, carbon capture, and membrane systems for amine regeneration environments.
